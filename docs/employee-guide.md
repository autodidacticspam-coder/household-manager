# Using Household Manager

## Sign in and language

Use the email address your administrator registered. Administrators sign in with a password. The current employee sign-in flow accepts a registered employee email and opens the employee account without an emailed verification code. Employees land on My Tasks; members of the Babysitter group land on availability and bookings.

Choose English, Spanish, or Chinese in Settings, or in Profile → Edit Profile. Wait for the language change to finish saving. The preference follows your account between devices and is used for push messages. Dish names, notes, and other text people enter remain their own content. Task details offer “Show original text” when a translated task is displayed.

## Tasks

My Tasks shows tasks you are assigned or allowed to view. A viewer can read a task; assignees can mark it in progress or complete. Administrators can edit its content and audience. Completing a task records the person who completed it.

Repeating tasks have individual dated occurrences. Editing or deleting future occurrences preserves completed task history. Administrators choose the weekdays, interval, and end date when creating a series.

## Food requests and meal ideas

On Food Ratings, choose Requests or the Requests count near the top. Pending requests appear first. Choose My requests to see only yours, or search by dish, notes, or requester. History contains completed and declined requests; it no longer takes space above pending requests. Your own pending requests have a Cancel action. Mark completed only when a request is fulfilled.

Administrators can create requests and view Insights. Administrators and chefs can open Meal ideas on the menu page. Suggestions show rating counts, how many people provided feedback, requests, and dated request completions. Filter by saved dish tags, copy a dish into the menu, or create a request where that action is available. Suggestions do not automatically change a menu or treat a draft plan as proof that a meal was served.

## Time off

Select the actual dates you need, including separate dates with gaps. Only selected dates count. A partial-day request uses one date with a start time and a later end time. Times use the existing eight-hour accounting day; approval records the exact debit in each affected calendar year.

Requests remain pending until reviewed by an administrator. Repeated approval does not charge your balance twice. Cancelling an approved request, where your account is permitted to do so, restores the recorded debit. An approved partial-day request shows you as away during its time window, rather than the entire day.

## Groups and access

Administrators manage employee groups. Chef membership enables menu and food-rating work; Nanny and Teacher memberships provide the applicable child-log and schedule access; Babysitter membership provides availability and booking tools. Your tasks and calendar exports respect their assigned audience.
